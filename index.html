POSTDOCTORAL RESEARCHER · CUHK-SHENZHEN

Weinan Guan 管伟楠

AI safety & multimedia forensics

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, with research conducted at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences. Since March 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen.

My research focuses on generalizable deepfake and AI-generated image detection, reliable multimedia forensics, and proactive media protection.
