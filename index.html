Research at the intersection of

AI safety &
multimedia forensics.

Building reliable and generalizable methods to detect manipulated and AI-generated media.

I have published first-authored research in IEEE TIFS, ICPR and PRCV, with further publications in ACM MM and Information Sciences. My work focuses on deepfake detection, cross-domain generalization, and the reliability of forensic decisions.

I am a postdoctoral researcher at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen. I received my Ph.D. from the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences in January 2025, with research conducted at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

Research interests

Generalizable detection

Reliable & multimodal evidence

Detection agents
